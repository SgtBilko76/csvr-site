Team Beef presents CSVR: Counter Strike 1.6 in VR.

Get ready to experience the most iconic tactical first-person shooter ever – on Quest and Pico standalone headsets. CSVR features multiplayer and single-player against bots!

This is a standalone port; a pc is only needed for installation. Tested on Quest 2/3/3s/Pro & Pico 3/4/4Ultra.

Install from your browser

Over USB, for Meta Quest and Pico: installs CSVR and copies the cstrike and valve folders from your own Counter-Strike 1.6 to the headset. No SideQuest needed. Works in Chrome or Edge on a PC, Mac or Android phone.

The installer needs JavaScript. Use the download button instead.

First time? Before you start
  1. Turn on developer mode. Quest: Meta Horizon app on your phone → Devices → your headset → Headset settings → Developer mode. Pico: Settings → General → About → tap Software version 7 times, then Settings → Developer → turn on USB debugging.
  2. Connect the headset to this computer with a USB cable that carries data (not a charge-only cable).
  3. Close SideQuest and Meta Quest Developer Hub. If you use adb, run adb kill-server first: only one program can talk to the headset at a time.
  4. Windows: if the headset doesn't show up in the list, install the Meta Quest ADB driver.
  5. After pressing Connect, put on the headset and allow USB debugging for this computer.

Features:

  • 6DoF controller tracking (aim with your sights or use the classic hud)
  • PvP online multiplayer
  • Off-line play against bots
  • Create your own servers directly from inside the headset!
  • Play with friends in your local wifi or online with players worldwide.
  • Crossplay between CS1.6 Xash Android and CSVR.
